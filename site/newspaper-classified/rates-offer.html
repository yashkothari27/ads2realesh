Pricing explained

What will my newspaper ad cost?

Four things set the price: the ad format, the newspaper, the city edition and the length or size. GST at 5% is added on top. You see the full amount before you pay.

Newspaper and edition

Each city edition has its own rate card, so one paper can charge different amounts in Pune and Mumbai.

Length or size

Text ads are charged per line or word, with a minimum. Boxed and display ads are width × height × the edition's rate per sq. cm.

Day, page and colour

Sunday editions, a fixed page and colour printing each add to the base rate.

Publishing timeline

How early do I need to book?

Most text classifieds booked and approved by 5 pm appear in the next morning's paper.

Choose your own date

Pick the publishing date when you book. The ad runs once the newspaper's ad desk approves the content.

Sunday papers

Sunday is the busiest day for matrimonial and job ads. Book at least two days ahead to be safe.

Display ads

We send a quote within the working day. Allow 2 to 3 days for artwork and page allocation, and more for a fixed position.
