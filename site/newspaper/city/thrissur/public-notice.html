How the price is worked out

  • Rate per word or line set by the newspaper for its Thrissur edition.
  • Minimum length: shorter ads are charged at the paper's minimum.
  • Extras such as bold, a tick mark, background colour or a border add a percentage.
  • GST at 5% is added. The total is shown before you pay.

Public notice ads in Thrissur: questions

How much does a public notice ad cost in Thrissur?

A 25-word ad costs between ₹455 and ₹525 per insertion before GST, depending on the newspaper.

When is the booking deadline?

Usually 5 pm the day before for text ads. Sunday editions close one or two days earlier, so book ahead for weekend dates.

Can I make my ad stand out?

Yes. Add bold text, a tick mark, a background colour or a border, or book a boxed ad with your logo.

Can you translate my ad?

Yes. Write it in English and our desk translates it into Hindi, Marathi or the paper's language for free, and shows you the proof first.
